Marketing engagement model

The R·E·D Method

A working consultation for coaching and consulting businesses — presented as the conversation itself.

R Reveal Your Offer · E Engage and Convert · D Develop Your Audience

The deck runs as one continuous consultation. Three stages; each stage ends with a written summary before moving on.

Opening

Client

I run a coaching practice. The work itself is solid — clients get results. The business development side is the part I haven't systematized. Content and outreach happen when there's time, which is usually never.

Consultant

That's typical, and it's workable. There are three areas to put in order: who the practice serves, how interest becomes enrollment, and how new people find it consistently. We'll take them in that order — each one makes the next easier.

Set the agenda in the client's language. Three areas: offer, conversion, audience. Sequence matters.
R

Reveal Your Offer

Define the practice: who it serves, what it delivers, how it is described, and how it is structured.

Stage R covers four modules: ideal client, offer currency, marketing message, curriculum.

Stage R · Reveal Your Offer

Consultant

Who does the practice serve best? Not the widest audience — the one that gets the strongest result from your work.

Client

I've kept it broad so far. Officially, anyone growing a business.

Consultant

Broad messaging reads as generic, so it gets ignored. We'll define the client profile specifically — role, frustrations, what they've already tried. It narrows the copy and sharpens it at the same time.

Consultant

Next: what does a client actually walk away with? Not sessions — the result. Time back, clearer positioning, revenue they can point to. That's the currency of the offer, and it's what the marketing should name.

Client

Time and confidence in their pricing. I hadn't written that down before.

Modules 1–2. Specific client profile, then the offer stated as a result. This gives all downstream copy its vocabulary.

Stage R · Reveal Your Offer

Consultant

Two more items. First, the message: one sentence a past client could repeat about what changed for them. Website, emails, and conversations all reuse it.

Client

One sentence, used everywhere. That alone would clean things up.

Consultant

Second, the program structure: sessions and materials organized into a curriculum with a clear path, so a prospect can see the whole engagement before starting. Understandable, and easy to agree to.

Modules 3–4. The offer is now defined and packaged — ready for the conversion stage.

Stage R · Written summary

ClientOne profile: role, frustrations, prior attempts.
CurrencyThe offer stated as the result delivered — time and pricing confidence, in this example.
MessageOne sentence, reused across site, email, and calls.
StructureThe program organized as a curriculum a prospect can review in advance.
Pause here. Stage R output feeds every later stage; nothing downstream works without it.
E

Engage and Convert

Build the path from first contact to enrollment: capture, follow-up, and the enrollment conversation.

Stage E covers three modules: lead magnet, funnel, prescriptive sales process.

Stage E · Engage and Convert

Consultant

Someone new discovers the practice. What do they receive first?

Client

At the moment, an empty contact form.

Consultant

We'll add a lead magnet: one useful resource that solves a small, real problem, exchanged for an email address. It demonstrates the work instead of describing it.

Consultant

Then the funnel around it: a landing page making one promise, a thank-you page that sets expectations, and scheduled follow-up. Every step leads somewhere specific.

Modules 1–2. Capture (magnet) then guidance (funnel). No dead ends.

Stage E · Engage and Convert

Client

The part I avoid is the sales conversation itself. I either talk too long or never ask for the decision.

Consultant

We'll replace improvisation with a process: first diagnose — where the prospect is, what they've tried, what outcome they need. Then prescribe — whether the program fits, stated plainly. It reads as a consultation because it is one.

Client

So the call has steps I can follow, and the decision comes from the fit.

Consultant

Correct. A clear no is a fine outcome. It's what makes the yeses reliable.

Module 3. Structure removes the pressure from selling: diagnosis, prescription, invitation.

Stage E · Written summary

CaptureA lead magnet that solves one small, real problem.
PathLanding page → thank-you page → scheduled follow-up. No dead ends.
EnrollmentA prescriptive call: diagnose, prescribe, invite. Fit decides.
Stage E output: interest now has a defined route to enrollment.
D

Develop Your Audience

Make discovery consistent: content, follow-up on interested visitors, and paid advertising.

Stage D covers three modules: content strategy, retargeting/remarketing, paid advertising.

Stage D · Develop Your Audience

Consultant

The conversion path is in place. Where do new people come from?

Client

Occasionally a referral. I post, but without a pattern.

Consultant

Content gets a schedule and a source: posts and emails drawn from the Stage R message and curriculum, so nothing is invented fresh each week. And visitors who don't opt in on the first visit get retargeted — the same message, shown again, at lower cost than a first impression.

Client

One message, many placements. That's manageable.

Modules 1–2. Content repurposes Stage R; remarketing recaptures visitors the funnel already attracted.

Stage D · Develop Your Audience

Consultant

Last item: paid advertising. Once the funnel converts at a known rate, ads are a scale mechanism — you know the value of a client, and you're buying attention below that value. We scale what's measured.

Client

So the order is: define, convert, then amplify.

Consultant

That's the method.

Module 3. Advertising amplifies a proven funnel; it doesn't fix an undefined one.

Stage D · Written summary

ContentScheduled posts and emails, all drawn from the Stage R message.
RetargetingInterested visitors re-shown the same message at lower cost.
AdvertisingPaid traffic scaled against a measured client value.
Stage D output: discovery is now systematic. R, E, D together form the full engagement model.
